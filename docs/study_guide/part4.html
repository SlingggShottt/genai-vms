7.2 The API — the one door

The API (FastAPI) is the only thing the browser talks to. It authenticates, checks permissions, forwards search and assistant calls to the retrieval service, signs video links, and pushes live updates. Three ways data reaches the browser:

  • REST (ordinary request/response) for almost everything;
  • WebSocket for live pushes: new alerts, job progress, "incident ready", camera status. The push is only a hint; the browser refetches the real state from the database, so a missed push never loses data;
  • SSE (server-sent events) to stream the assistant's answer as it is written.

Errors always use one envelope {"error": {"code", "message", "details", "request_id"}}, which makes the UI's messages consistent.

7.3 Security and roles

  • Passwords are hashed; login returns short-lived JWT tokens. The API re-reads the user and role from the database on every call, so changing a role or deactivating a user takes effect immediately.
  • Three roles (admin, operator, viewer). A role × endpoint test matrix checks every endpoint against every role, so a new endpoint cannot silently be open to everyone.
  • Rate limits in Redis: login 10 per minute, search 30, assistant 20, analyses and reports 6.
  • Presigned links for video and pictures expire in 15 minutes and are created per request; permanent links are never stored.
  • CORS allow-list, security headers and a Content-Security-Policy; a secret scanner (gitleaks) passes on the repo; secrets come only from the environment.
  • Deliberately out of scope: face recognition and face blurring.

9.4 Retrieval quality on real labels (MEVA activity clips)

Fast mode puts a relevant window at rank 1 for 5 of 36 queries and in the top 10 for 20 (random would be about 1 and 9). Reason mode: 8 and 24, at roughly 70 times the latency; query by query it was better on 12, worse on 7. What it shows: search is good at what is in the picture (phones, laptops, bikes, trunks, 7 of 7 in the top 10) and poor at actions that need motion (turning left, handing over, entering through a door).

Be honest about…The clips are short and staged with black gaps between them, so these numbers are a ceiling, not a promise for a day of one real camera. One query per activity, written by the builder. A newer, larger harness (63 queries, explicit vs implicit, four configurations, mAP and tIoU) is built and tested, but its final clean run did not finish: the first attempts were invalidated by a recording gap and stale data, which taught us useful lessons (chapter 10).

9.5 The fine-tuning experiment — the honest headline

We trained a QLoRA adapter for the phase locator on the real 3B model, on the 4 GB laptop GPU, in about 50 minutes (138 training samples; 26 validation; 32 held-out test samples from 18 clips). We scored it on the held-out set.

  • Overall the adapter looks better (0.354 vs 0.274), and the paired difference is small but positive (+0.080, 95% interval +0.005 to +0.153).
  • But the whole gain is on the 8 samples whose labels came from a padding rule (+0.265): the adapter learned the labelling rule. On a person's labels (6 samples) it was slightly worse (−0.04) and on drafts that looked at the clip it was about equal (+0.02), both with intervals that include zero.
  • What it does win: it always answers in the right form (32 of 32 usable answers vs 23 of 32), puts boundaries 1 to 2 seconds nearer, and labels more frames correctly (0.61 vs 0.47).
  • It stayed well below the detector's own timing on judged labels, and it almost never says "precursor" or "escalation". So we did not make it the default.
How to say it"We proved the pipeline: we can fine-tune a 3B vision model on a 4 GB laptop and serve it. But with labels that are mostly model drafts, we cannot claim it understands incident phases better. A fair test needs more labels made by a person, which is the next step."
Be honest about…Labels: of 119 labelled clips, a person marked 9 from scratch and checked 20 drafts; 58 are model drafts made from contact sheets, 25 come from a padding rule, 7 from the dataset's crime span. Test samples from one clip (its 2 or 3 camera views) are not independent, so the intervals are tighter than they should be. One run, one seed.

9.6 Assistant and report quality

72 test questions were asked before and after fixes. Objective checks (no model judging): the first lookup was the right tool 78% → 100%; the lookup used the camera asked about 28% → 100%; used the day asked about 0% → 100%; tags that matched no record fell from 27/92 to 14/80.
