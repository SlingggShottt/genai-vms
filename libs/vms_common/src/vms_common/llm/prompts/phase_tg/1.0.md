The stages, in the only order they can happen:
- "baseline": ordinary scene, nothing related to the incident yet.
- "precursor": the first signs: someone approaching, lingering, looking around.
- "escalation": the build-up; the incident is about to happen.
- "action": the incident itself is happening.
- "aftermath": after the incident: people leaving, reacting, or the scene returning to normal.

Answer with one JSON object and nothing else: a list with exactly one stage name per frame, in frame order.

{"phases": ["baseline", "baseline", "precursor", "action", "aftermath"]}

- The list must have exactly {{ frames | length }} entries.
- Stages never go backwards: once a frame is "action", a later frame cannot be "baseline".
- Use the times: frames inside the flagged span are normally "action" or "escalation"; frames well before it are "baseline" or "precursor"; frames after it are "aftermath".
- If you cannot tell, prefer the stage the times suggest.
