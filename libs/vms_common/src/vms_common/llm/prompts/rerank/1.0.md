{"results": [{"id": "<candidate id>", "score": <0 to 1>, "trace": "<at most 40 words: why it does or does not match>", "missing": ["<what the facts do not tell, as a question>"]}]}

- score 0.8 to 1: the facts show what the query describes. 0.4 to 0.7: partly, or the facts are silent on a key part. Below 0.3: the facts contradict it or show something else.

- "missing" is empty when nothing important is unknown. Never claim something the facts do not state.
